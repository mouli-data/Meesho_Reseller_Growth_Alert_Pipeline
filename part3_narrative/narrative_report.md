A bar chart communicates the comparison clearly within 10 seconds. The y-axis starts at zero, 3D charts are avoided, and no legend is needed because there is only one series.

### 3. Region Revenue Comparison

Use this to compare total revenue across the four regions.

**Reason:** This is a **univariate** comparison because it examines one measure, total revenue, across different regions. A bar chart makes the differences between regions clear within 10 seconds. ### General Chart Rules

- Keep the y-axis starting at zero.
- Avoid 3D charts.
- Use a legend only when multiple series require it.
- Choose the simplest chart that communicates the business message clearly within 10 seconds.
